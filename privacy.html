Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

BMI Calculator is published by M4Quick LLC. This policy explains what information is collected when you use this site, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. The calculator does not collect your data

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The height and weight you enter are used to perform the arithmetic on your own device and are never transmitted to us, never stored, and never logged. Closing the page discards them.

2. Information you give us

The only way to send us personal information directly is by emailing us. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and we use it solely to reply and act on your message. We do not add you to mailing lists and we do not sell or share it.

3. Technical data

Like most websites, our hosting and advertising providers automatically receive standard technical information when a page is requested — browser type, device type, approximate location derived from IP address, and referring page. We use this only in aggregate.

4. Cookies and advertising

This site displays advertising served by Google, and Google's advertising products use cookies and similar technologies. Specifically:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info/choices.
  • See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. See our cookie policy for the breakdown.

5. Analytics

We may use basic analytics to understand aggregate site usage. This data is aggregated, does not identify you personally, and never includes anything you type into the calculator.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information a site holds about you. Because we maintain no user accounts and the calculator stores nothing, in most cases we hold no personal information about you beyond any email correspondence. If you have emailed us and would like that deleted, contact us.

7. Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Adult BMI categories do not apply to children and teenagers, who are assessed on age- and sex-specific percentile charts by a paediatric provider.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, reach us via our contact page.

Privacy enquiries can also be posted to M4Quick LLC, 440 Monticello Ave Ste 1802 PMB 574599, Norfolk, Virginia 23510-2670 US, though email reaches us considerably faster.
